TRACKING AI
Computer Vision
People Counting & Tracking System

An end-to-end Deep Learning tracking system designed for crowd management, monitoring occupancy limits by automatically counting people entering or exiting a designated area based on a configured reference line.

Tracking Methodology:
  • Object Detection: Detects individuals within a video frame using YOLOv3 bounding box identification.
  • Centroid Tracking: Registers and computes the geometric centroid for each person, and assigns a constant unique Object ID.
  • Continuous Monitoring: Calculates Euclidean distance across successive frames to map similarity metrics, tracking individuals reliably.
  • In/Out Operations: Evaluates directional flow relative to the reference line, incrementing up/down counters in real-time.
